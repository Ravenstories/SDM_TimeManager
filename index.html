Your time, your data.

Time continues until you pause or switch, including while minimized, asleep, or closed. On your return, elapsed time is calculated from the saved start time. Your device's local timezone determines each day.

Time and notes are saved automatically in a database in this browser. They are not published to GitHub. There is no cloud sync. Download backups to keep a copy outside this browser, or to move your history to another device.

Protection can reduce automatic browser cleanup, but it cannot protect against clearing site data, losing this device, or private browsing. Keep an external backup.

External backups

Restoring replaces local records and pauses an imported running timer at its export time. A recovery point preserves your current records first.

Recovery points

Up to 14 snapshots are kept in this browser: before the first change each day and before a restore. These help undo a restore but are not an external backup.

A recovery point will be created when you first track time or save a note.

Keep it close

Pin this tab or use your browser's install / open as app option where available. Compact view keeps both clocks within easy reach. After the first online visit, the app can open offline.

Keyboard shortcuts work while this app has focus, outside text fields. They are not global desktop shortcuts.

Additional clocks

Add as many clocks as you need for ad hoc work, meetings, or breaks. They appear three per row on larger screens. Removing one keeps its history.
