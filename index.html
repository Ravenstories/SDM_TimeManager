        Browser storage

        Time and notes are saved in this browser. Download a backup to move them to another device.

        Protection reduces automatic cleanup. Keep a separate backup in case site data is cleared or the device is lost.

        Recovery points

        The app keeps up to 14 snapshots in this browser, including one before your first change each day and before a restore.

        A recovery point will appear here after you start tracking or save a note.

        How tracking and shortcuts work

        Your timer keeps counting after you close the window or put your device to sleep. Use Pause tracking when you stop working. The app uses your device’s local time for each day.

        While the app has focus, press 1–9 to switch clocks or Space to pause. You can also pin or install the app if your browser offers that option.
